❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Tap any question to see answer
How to post a FREE Ad? +
Tap on + Sell button at bottom, fill Title, Price, City, Area and Photo, select FREE plan and tap Publish Ad. Your Ad will be live instantly.
Is posting Ad really FREE? +
Yes! FREE Lifetime plan is 100% FREE forever. No hidden charges. Featured plans 499 and 999 are only if you want more views.
How to contact seller? +
Open any Ad, tap Call or WhatsApp button to talk directly to seller. You can also tap Chat to message inside app.
How to pay for Featured Ad? +
Select 499 or 999 plan, pay via Easypaisa and upload payment screenshot. Admin will approve your Featured Ad within few hours.
How to delete my Ad? +
Go to Profile > My Ads, tap on your Ad and tap Delete. Your Ad will be removed immediately.
How to talk to Support? +
Go to Profile > Help & Support button and send message. Our team will reply you as soon as possible.
