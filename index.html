❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. How to post an Ad?

Login first, then tap Sell button, fill title, price, photo and tap Post Ad. It is 100% FREE.

2. How to make my Ad Featured?

Go to My Ads, tap SELL FAST NOW, select plan, pay on Easypaisa and upload screenshot. Admin will approve within 1-2 hours.

3. How to contact seller?

Open Ad detail, you can Call or WhatsApp seller.

4. How to edit or delete my Ad?

Go to My Ads page, tap Edit or Delete. Only your own Ads can be edited.

5. Is it safe to buy?

Always meet in a public place, check item before payment. Never send advance payment to unknown person.
